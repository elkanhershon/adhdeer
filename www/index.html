How to use this planner

A planner to help you get things done — nothing complicated, just your stuff, planned the way you need.

The four kinds of things

To do — something you need to do. Leave the time empty and I'll find a gap; set a time (or tap one on the calendar) to fix it in place. Star (★) means important, and important things get the best gaps.

Event — happens at a set time (a class, a meeting). Pick the day, the start time and how long it lasts.

Deadline — a moment something is due. Add it, then tap Split it up and I'll spread the work over the days before it, around the things already in your calendar.

Habit — something you repeat, like reading 20 pages. Pick your days and tick it off as you go. Your streak grows with every day you keep it up.

A normal day

1. Add what you need to do. Times sort themselves out — you don't have to plan.

2. Look at today. Everything is colour-coded and labelled, and tapping a block opens it for editing.

3. Change your mind? Rearrange with the Rearrange the day button — fixed things stay put, everything else shuffles around them.

When something feels too big

Type it and press Split it up. I'll break it into smaller steps and schedule each one. If you're not sure how long it takes, press Guess the time.

Saving and moving devices

Your planner lives in this browser. Deleting your browsing data will remove it, so keep backups.

Save a backup file gives you a copy to keep anywhere (Drive, iCloud). Load a backup file brings it back on a new device.

If you have a home storage box (NAS), set it up once in Settings, then Save to my home storage at the end of a session and Load from it when you start on another device. The newest copy wins, so sync between edits, not after.
